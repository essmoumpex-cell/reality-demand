    SAFE PATH

    Smallest possible investment.

    Run a controlled test before buying stock or equipment.

    LOW INVESTMENT · LOW RISK

    GROWTH PATH

    Reinvest after real customers validate the offer.

    Increase supply and distribution gradually.

    MEDIUM INVESTMENT · MEDIUM RISK

    AGGRESSIVE PATH

    Scale stock, channels and marketing.

    Only use this after strong demand and positive unit economics.

    HIGH INVESTMENT · HIGH RISK

    7-Day Reality Test

    From research to decision
    DAY 1
    Define the exact customer, problem and offer.
    DAY 2
    Map competitors and record what they actually sell.
    DAY 3
    Record real prices and compare your target price.
    DAY 4
    Speak with 10 real target customers. Ask what they currently buy, what they pay and what they dislike.
    DAY 5
    Run the smallest real-world paid test possible.
    DAY 6
    Measure sales, costs, conversion, complaints and repeat interest.
    DAY 7
    Choose: SCALE · TEST AGAIN · CHANGE · STOP.

    Final Decision

    Reality, not hype
